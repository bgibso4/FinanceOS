Outside budget

"Transfer" has been doing two jobs. Sometimes it means money moving between your own accounts. Sometimes it means "real spending I don't want wrecking a normal month", like the Green Card fees. Because both hide the money everywhere, the Green Card tag showed $0. On top of that, bank sync hides anything the bank calls a transfer, even when nothing in your other accounts matches it, so some everyday payments and income vanish without you choosing. This gives "outside budget" its own checkbox, makes Transfer mean only transfers again, and stops sync hiding payments it can't match. (Issue #51.)

Scope

Goals

  • Keep a real cost out of your monthly numbers without hiding it
  • The Green Card tag shows what it cost
  • Sync stops hiding payments nobody confirmed were transfers
  • Tags collapsed in the filter panel

Non-goals

  • The Review tab (needs its own overhaul)
  • Remembering a decision per payee
  • Budgeting for non-monthly costs (#50)
  • Bulk-editing your 30 existing rows

Anti-goals

  • Two spending views that disagree about what counts
  • A checkbox that draws attention on every transaction
  • Undoing a transfer you marked by hand on a later sync
  • Breaking the pairing of real transfers between your accounts

1 · The flag and where it counts

  • New field Transaction.isOutsideBudget Boolean @default(false), applied with prisma db push. migrate dev fails on this database.
  • One rule in src/lib/analytics.ts: loadPeriodTransactions and monthlySnapshot leave out isOutsideBudget rows unless the filters say to include them. The Analytics toggle, tag totals and Reports → Cash Flow ask to include them. Everything else uses the default (M1).
  • Cash Flow mixes live figures (/api/analytics/dashboard) with month-close snapshots (/api/reports/trailing-12-months). Both parts must count outside-budget rows, so a closed month and an open month agree. The builder decides how: store both totals in the snapshot, or recompute.
  • It works the same for money coming in. A one-off refund or gift marked outside budget is left out of the totals in the same way.
  • The PATCH and bulk transaction APIs accept the field. Setting isTransfer: true clears it, and a request setting both to true is refused (M4).
  • The cloud-sync payload carries the field, so a pull on another device keeps it.

2 · Drawer and list

  • DetailsSection: the last line of the card, below Trip, is a tick box labelled Outside budget, separated by a hairline. When it's ticked, a muted line underneath reads "Left out of your monthly numbers". It's hidden while the row is a Transfer. Ticking saves straight away and reloads the page's totals, the same way the Transfer switch does.
  • Split parts each have their own tick box. A split parent doesn't show one, because it's never counted.
  • TransactionTable: an outside-budget row reads Merchant · Outside budget, with the suffix in muted text. Category, colours and amount are unchanged.

3 · Analytics and tags

  • The query gains an outside flag, written to the address as outside=1 only when it's on, so existing links are unchanged. parseAnalyticsQuery and toSearchParams round-trip it.
  • Filter panel: a new section below Tags, with the label Include outside budget, the hint "Spending you kept out of your monthly numbers", and a switch. It counts towards the funnel badge when on, and "Clear all" turns it off.
  • Chips: Incl. outside budget ✕ appears while it's on, styled like the other chips you add (faint cognac edge).
  • Tags starts collapsed like Categories and Accounts, with what's picked shown in its header (A6).
  • spendingByTag always includes outside-budget rows, and tagCostHref adds outside=1, so the Settings total and the page it links to agree (M2).

4 · Bank sync

  • The aggregator's TRANSFER_IN / TRANSFER_OUT label stops setting isTransfer on its own. A row becomes a transfer when the existing cross-account pairing in sync-common.ts matches it with a transaction in another of your accounts, or when you mark it.
  • Pairing must still work when the two sides arrive in different syncs (the second bank posts a day later). The earlier side is now an ordinary transaction, and the later sync has to find it and pair them.
  • Anything already marked as a transfer, by you or by pairing, is never changed back by a later sync.
  • When one bank is slow, the first side to arrive is an ordinary transaction until its partner shows up. The next sync pairs them, as long as their dates are at most 3 days apart (1 day without a "transfer"/"payment"-type word) and the amounts match. Pairing that makes a row a transfer also clears its outside-budget tick (M4).
  • Existing special cases (Venmo person payments, card top-ups, brokerage deposits) carry on as before.

5 · Your 30 existing rows

  • No data change ships with the PR (M5).
  • After merging, I give you the list: the 22 payments out and 8 deposits in that are marked as transfers with no match, oldest first, Balance Adjustments excluded. You open each in the drawer.
  • For the Green Card fees and Manifest Legal, the likely steps are Transaction, then tick Outside budget, then (if you want) the Green Card tag.
