Why

Travel is a large part of your life, and today trips are tracked with tags.

7 of 9tags are trips
227trip-tagged transactions
94%of trip spend is in the Travel categories
$2.2kTravel spend on no trip: small trips left untagged
  1. Tagging is one transaction at a time. Flights and lodging are booked 2 weeks to 4 months early, and settle-ups and refunds arrive up to 3 weeks after, so finding a trip's transactions means hunting.
  2. The tag list grows with every trip, crowding out the tags that aren't trips.
  3. Analysis goes through the Finance Analyst. "Compare the costs of my trips this year, with a category breakdown of each" should be built in.
  4. Dates alone are not enough. About a third of the transactions inside a trip's dates are salary, gym, subscriptions or groceries at home.
  5. The app can't see money paid to friends. Adrian's "Whis" −$1,280 is just a Lodging charge.

Scope

What this does, what it leaves out, and what would make it a failure even if it works.

Goals

  • Trips as first-class records (name, dates, note)
  • Adding transactions by review, not hunt
  • A Travel page: the year's trips and the budget
  • A trip page with every transaction editable
  • Travel owns the yearly budget

Non-goals

  • Side-by-side trip comparison
  • Auto-assigning transactions, or AI
  • Detecting money paid to friends
  • Analytics' modal → drawer (follow-up)
  • Other date ranges and category-over-time (separate projects)

Anti-goals

  • A read-only transaction list
  • A new colour palette
  • A budget prompt that nags

Constraints

  • One transaction editor. Every row in Travel opens the existing TransactionDrawer, fully editable, with ‹ › stepping through the rows shown. No bespoke editor, no modal.
  • Push drawers over modals: the existing Drawer with DRAWER_PUSH_CLASS.
  • Design tokens only (src/app/globals.css). Everything works in light and dark.
  • Schema changes use prisma db push, not migrate dev, which fails with P3006 here.
  • Cloud sync lists each table explicitly (src/lib/cloud-sync/sync.ts, types.ts). Every new table and column goes into export, import and the Zod payload, or it's silently lost on another device.
  • Vendor-neutral naming in domain code. Amounts: negative = spend, positive = money in. Dates in UTC.
  • Net, not gross M1 · converted to base currency M2
  • PR against main; no stacked PRs.

1 · Data model

  • Trip: id, name, startDate, endDate (inclusive calendar days), createdAt. No note field, which nobody asked for.
  • Transaction → trip: each transaction is on at most one trip, via a nullable trip reference on Transaction. Deleting a trip clears it from its transactions and keeps them.
  • Dismissed suggestions: the (trip, transaction) pairs left unticked in a review.
  • Split transactions: the parts are assigned and counted, never the split parent (isSplitParent: false, as goals do).

2 · Converting the existing trip tags

A one-off script, run once. Its writes reach other devices through normal sync.

  1. Tags matching ^\d{4} - become trips M4: Austria/Italy, Bayfield, California, Costa Rica, Miami, Mt Tremblant and Whistler. The "2026 - " prefix is dropped and each tag's transactions are assigned to its trip.
  2. Dates come from the trip's Travel-category spending, excluding Flights and Lodging because those are booked early. If there's none, they come from all of its transactions. The dry run lists every guessed date, and you correct any wrong ones with Edit trip. Nothing extra is stored for this.
  3. A backup, a dry-run summary, and safe to run twice M5.
  4. The converted tags are removed from their transactions and deleted. No goal may be left pointing at a deleted tag. The "2026 Travel Budget" switches to the new tracking method.

3 · Suggestions

Step 2 of creating a trip, and what "Review suggestions" opens. Candidates are not on any trip, not transfers between your own accounts, not split parents, and not dismissed for this trip.

GroupRulePre-ticked
Booked beforeFlights or Lodging in the 4 months before the start dateYes
DuringTravel category group, dated start–endYes
Other spending duringAny other spending dated start–end, except income and recurring transactions such as subscriptionsNo
After: TravelTravel-category transactions in the 3 weeks after the end date M3Yes
After: other money inAny other incoming money in those 3 weeks, except income categories and recurring. Friend paybacks land here. M3No

How the list behaves

  • Each row shows the merchant, date, category, amount and why it's suggested (e.g. "46 days before"). The footer button is Add N transactions, where N is the number ticked, or Dismiss all when nothing is ticked.
  • Unticked rows are dismissed for that trip M7.
  • Review suggestions · N on the trip page reopens the list with only new candidates: ones not yet added or dismissed, such as a late rental-car bill. The button is hidden at 0.
  • Editing a trip's dates keeps what you added and what you dismissed M6.
  • Nothing joins a trip without a tick.

4 · Travel page /travel

Mock A.

  • Header: a "Travel" caption, the year as the title, the year chip A5, and + New trip.
  • Three cards
    • Travel budget: spent, target, a bar (red when over), "$X over" or "$X left". With no budget for the year, it offers to set one.
    • Trips: the number of trips starting that year and their total days.
    • vs last year: this year's budget-spent figure against last year's, or "—" with a note unless history reaches January 1 of last year, so a partial year is never compared.
  • Trip list, biggest total first: Trip · Dates · Where it went · Per day · Total.
    • Where it went is a split bar in one hue at decreasing strength A4. A legend names the top three with their percentages. There are no hover details.
    • Per day = total ÷ days (inclusive). A row opens the trip page.
  • Not on a trip: a row under the trips for Travel-category spending that year on no trip, with total and count. It opens a list whose rows open the transaction drawer.

5 · Trip page /travel/[id]

Mock C.

  • Header: a "Travel · 2026" caption, the name, dates and days, Edit trip, and Review suggestions · N when N > 0.
  • Four cards
    • Total: net, with the transaction count.
    • Per day: with the year's average across trips.
    • Booked ahead: spend dated before the start.
    • Money back: incoming money on the trip, already taken off the total.
  • By category: bars in the same tonal scale, biggest first. Each category is net of its credits, so the list adds up to the Total. A fully refunded category drops out; one that nets to a credit shows a negative amount and no bar.
  • Transactions: the Transactions table's columns (Date, Merchant, Category, Amount), grouped into Before / During / After. A click pushes in the shared transaction drawer A3. The open row is highlighted, and ‹ › step through the trip's transactions.

7 · Transaction drawer change

  • DetailsSection gains a Trip field: a select of trips, most recent first, plus "None". It saves immediately, like category and tags.
  • That's how a transaction joins or leaves a trip from anywhere, including the Transactions page and the review queue.

8 · Travel budget

Travel owns it A6. It's still stored as a Goal, so progress and pace come from src/lib/goals.ts, through a new tracking method.

  • What it counts A7 A8: every transaction on a trip starting in that year, whatever its date, plus every Travel-category transaction on no trip dated in that year, each counted once. So budget spent = that year's trip totals + Not on a trip, and the page adds up.
  • A booking made before its trip exists counts in its own year under Not on a trip, and moves to the trip's year once it's added.
  • Only the budget works this way. Analytics, the other goals and Reports keep counting by transaction date.
  • Managed in Travel: one budget per calendar year, amount only. Goals lists it as "Managed in Travel" with a link, and it can't be edited there.
  • Yearly prompt: with no budget for the current year, a slim app-wide banner says "Set your <year> travel budget" and links to Travel. Not now hides it M8. It disappears once a budget is set.

9 · Finance Analyst

  • queryTransactions filters by tag today. It also needs to filter by trip, and the analyst needs a way to list trips, so trip questions keep working once trip tags are gone.

10 · Engineering

DRY > YAGNI > SOLID, in that order A9. Every task in the plan is judged against this.

DRY: extract, don't copy

  • Shared TransactionTable, pulled out of transactions/page.tsx: the Date / Merchant / Category / Account / Amount rows, the open-row highlight, and optional group headings for Before / During / After. Transactions and the trip page both use it, and Analytics will later.
  • A shared drawer hook, pulled out of the Transactions page: the drawer's state and handle, open, close, step through the shown list, update a row in place after a save, and refresh. Any page with a transaction list gets the drawer from this hook.
  • A shared period picker, pulled out of Analytics' month picker, with a month mode and a year mode. Analytics switches to it too.
  • One budget calculation. The Travel budget card and Goals both call calculateGoalProgress. The travel tracking method gets its yearly figure from TravelService, the same code that builds the page's trip totals and Not on a trip, so the card and the page can't disagree.
  • One "categories in a group" helper, pulled out of goals.ts (a group means its child categories). The category goal, suggestions and the budget all use it.
  • Reuse what exists: recurring transactions come from src/lib/recurring.ts, currency from getCurrencySettings / convertAmount, and the push drawer is the existing Drawer.

YAGNI: cut

  • The trip Note field.
  • "Check these dates" and any stored "dates confirmed" flag. The conversion's dry run covers it.
  • A conversion that runs on first visit. It's a script.
  • Hover details on the bar segments.
  • Interfaces with only one implementation, such as a trip repository.
  • Kept on purpose: the dismissed-suggestions table, because without it the review count could never reach 0.

SOLID: shape of src/lib/travel/

  • TripSuggester runs a list of suggestion rule objects, one per group: Before, During, Other during, After. Each rule says which transactions it matches, whether they're pre-ticked, and why. Adding a group means adding a rule, not editing a switch. This is the same pattern as RecurringDetectionStrategy.
  • TravelService handles trip totals, per day, Money back, booked ahead, the year view, Not on a trip, and the budget's yearly figure. The database, clock and currency settings are passed in.
  • createTravelServices is the one place these are wired together, like createInvestmentServices. Routes take services from it, and tests pass in a fixed clock and a test database instead of mocking modules.
  • Goal tracking becomes a lookup table with one calculator per method (category, tag, account, travel), replacing the if/else chain in goals.ts. Travel's calculator plugs in without touching the others.
  • Each file does one job: rules, service, routes and components stay separate. Pages render, and they don't calculate.

Left to the builder

  • API shape, file layout, component split, and whether the trip list and trip page share loaders.
  • How the shades come from --accent. The mock uses color-mix.
  • Empty states: no trips in a year, a trip with no transactions.
