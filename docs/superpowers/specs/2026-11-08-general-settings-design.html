General settings and automatic inflation

Settings → General becomes a proper settings screen: grouped rows that read at a glance and take new settings without a redesign. Exchange rates and inflation get real room — today's figure, a past-year sparkline you can hover, and a 12-month table a click away. Inflation stops being typed in: the official monthly consumer price index for the US and Canada is fetched automatically, and Reports' "Inflation-Adjusted" view uses it month by month, so it shows what your net worth really grew by.

Scope

Goals

  • General reads as a clean settings screen that takes new settings easily
  • Exchange rates and inflation are easy to read, with history on hover and a click away
  • US and Canadian inflation arrive automatically every month
  • Reports' inflation-adjusted net worth is exact to the month

Non-goals

  • Redesigning the other Settings tabs
  • Inflation anywhere other than Reports' snapshots
  • Countries beyond the US and Canada
  • Choosing exchange-rate pairs by hand (they still come from your accounts)
  • Category-level or regional price indexes

Anti-goals

  • A failed inflation fetch blocking account sync, or wiping stored months
  • Base currency or the inflation choice changing without you confirming
  • Clicking a row to see its history and accidentally changing a setting
  • Tests calling the real statistics APIs

Constraints

  • Follow the approved mock. Layout, wording, column order and behaviour as in round 5; real data replaces its samples.
  • Design tokens only, light and dark. No emoji in the General tab. Muted style: accent only for the sparkline, the selected radio and the "In use" tag.
  • DRY > YAGNI > SOLID. One sparkline component for every row; one row component for rates and inflation if it stays simple; one index service, not one per country with copied logic.
  • Vendor names only at the edges (BLS, Statistics Canada inside the source adapters; "consumer price index" elsewhere). The page may name the source in the table's footnote.
  • Tests never touch prisma/dev.db and never call a real statistics API, bank or price provider.
  • Schema changes via prisma db push; the live database only with Ben, after a backup.

1 · The General page

One column, about 760px wide, under the existing Settings tabs. Three groups, each a small uppercase caption over a card.

GroupRows
CurrencyBase currency (label, one-line explanation, select) · a header row · one row per exchange-rate pair · footer "Updated automatically · as of 7:20 am" with a quiet Refresh
InflationA header row · US row · Canada row · footer "Selected index is used by Reports' inflation-adjusted view · fetched monthly"
AppearanceTheme with a Light / Dark / System switch M2

Save bar

  • Appears at the bottom of the column, sticky, when base currency or the inflation choice differs from what is saved. It says the change in words, e.g. "Base currency → CAD · Reports adjusts by Canada inflation", with Discard (outline) and Save changes (the only accent button). M1
  • Save writes both at once and the bar goes; Discard restores the select and the radio.
  • Leaving the tab with unsaved changes discards them. my call

2 · Currency

  • Base currency keeps its four choices (USD, CAD, EUR, GBP), as "USD · US Dollar".
  • One row per pair the app already stores (from your accounts' currencies and the base currency). Columns: pair ("1 CAD → USD"), today's rate to 4 places, a past-year sparkline of weekly points, and the 1-year change in percent (red when down, green when up), then a chevron.
  • Sparkline points: the last stored rate in each week of the past year. 1-year change: today vs the rate for the same day a year ago (through the usual "latest at or before" lookup); "—" when history is shorter than a year. my call
  • Hover shows "Sep 18 · 1 CAD = 0.7046 USD".
  • Click opens a table: the last 12 months, newest first, each month's last stored rate ("Nov 2026 (so far)" for the current one), and a footnote that every day's rate is stored and each amount converts at its own day's rate.
  • Refresh runs the existing rate refresh; "as of" updates. A failed refresh keeps the existing message.
  • No pairs yet: one quiet line, "No exchange rates yet".

3 · Inflation

Where it comes from

  • The monthly consumer price index for each country, stored as index values per (country, month). M3
  • Backfill once from the earliest month Reports needs (the first net-worth snapshot, minus 12 months so the first annual figure can be shown), then each pass adds new months.
  • A month is published about two weeks after it ends; nothing is estimated in between.
  • A fetch that fails or returns nonsense (non-numbers, ≤ 0, or a month-to-month jump far beyond normal) writes nothing; the step counts as failed and stored months stay. M4
  • Published figures can be revised; re-reading the last few months on each fetch and overwriting them is fine.

The rows

  • Columns: radio · name ("US", "Canada") with a small "In use" tag on the saved choice · annual rate for the latest month · a 12-month sparkline of annual rates · latest month ("Sep 2026") · chevron.
  • Annual = index vs the same month a year earlier. Monthly = change from the month before. Both to one decimal place.
  • Radio: picking one moves the radio immediately; "In use" stays on the saved choice until Save. Clicking the radio never opens or closes the table. A7
  • Hover shows "Jun 2026 · 2.9% annual".
  • Click (anywhere but the radio) opens a table of the last 12 months, newest first: Month · Annual · Monthly, and a footnote naming the source and when it was fetched.
  • Default choice: US. A6
  • Before the first fetch: the rows say "Not fetched yet"; no sparkline. The first pass runs about 30 seconds after the server starts, so this is brief. There is no separate inflation Refresh. my call

4 · Reports

  • The "Inflation-Adjusted" toggle and the "Adjusted" column stay as they are. A8
  • A snapshot is restated by index(latest month) ÷ index(snapshot's month) using the saved country. A snapshot newer than the latest index uses factor 1.
  • With the toggle on, the view says the reference month, e.g. "In September 2026 prices". M6
  • The toggle shows only when index data exists for the saved country, as it does today with rates.
  • The factor is just the ratio of the chosen country's index, applied to totals in base currency; the country and base currency are independent choices. my call

5 · Engineering

  • One index service fills and reads monthly index rows for both countries through a small source interface (one adapter per statistics agency — two real implementations, so the seam earns its place).
  • A pass step inflation runs it, alongside the rate step; the Settings page doesn't fetch on load. A failed step shows in the existing "couldn't update …" line and side-nav dot like any other step, and clears after the next clean pass (the next pass retries 4 hours later). my call
  • src/lib/inflation.ts moves from yearly rates to monthly index lookups; the yearly-rate code and its tests go. M5
  • Storage: a new table for (country, month, index) replacing InflationRate; UserSettings gains the saved country (default US).
  • APIs: one read route returns, per country, the last 13 months of index values (enough for 12 annual and monthly figures) and the fetch time; the exchange-rate read gains enough history for the sparkline, 1-year change and month-end table. Shapes are the builder's call.
  • Cloud backup: the index is fetched, not personal data — leave it out of the backup, as InflationRate is today; UserSettings is already backed up, so the saved country goes with it (older backups without it restore as US).
  • The Settings page file is 4,600 lines; the General tab moves into its own component file.

Left to the builder

  • Exact table and field names, and how a month is stored (first-of-month UTC date or "YYYY-MM").
  • The Statistics Canada vector and API call for all-items CPI, and the BLS API version (v1 needs no key; its daily limit is ample for this).
  • How the step decides a new month could exist, and how far back to re-read for revisions.
  • The "far beyond normal" bound for a month-to-month jump.
  • Whether the sparkline is built on Recharts or plain SVG, as long as there's one component.
  • Whether the rate and inflation rows share a component.
  • Keyboard: the radio and rows reachable and operable by keyboard; the table opens with Enter/Space.

Testing

Unit

  • Annual and monthly figures from index values
  • Reports factor: same month = 1, older month, newer-than-latest = 1
  • Source adapters parse recorded API responses; nonsense is rejected
  • 1-year change and month-end table from rate rows

Integration

  • The inflation step backfills, adds a new month, and writes nothing on failure, with a fake source
  • A failed inflation step doesn't stop the other steps and is recorded
  • Saving base currency and the inflation choice persists both
  • Old inflation routes are gone

Component

  • Rows show rate, change and latest figures; hover shows a point
  • Clicking a row opens its table; clicking the radio doesn't
  • Save bar appears, Discard restores, Save persists
  • Reports shows the reference month with the toggle on
