Analytics: any period, with filters

Analytics stops being stuck on one calendar month. You pick a month, three months, a year or a custom range, step through them with arrows like today, and narrow everything on the page with filters. That answers questions like "how much did I spend on golf this year, compared with last year?" in a few clicks.

Why

You want to see spending over more than one month, and drill into categories. "Improving this will pay dividends for the usability of this page."

  1. The page is stuck on one calendar month. Questions like "golf this year" or "clothes since summer" can't be answered here.
  2. The comparison is fixed to the month before, shown as a percentage.
  3. There's no way to narrow to a category, a group, a card, a tag or a merchant.
  4. The server can already do much of it. /api/analytics/dashboard takes any date range and parses account, category, tag and merchant filters; the page just never asks.

Scope

Goals

  • Month, 3 months, year and custom periods, stepped with arrows
  • A chosen comparison: period before, same time last year, or none
  • Filters: category (groups and categories), account, tag, merchant
  • An over-time line chart, including one line per category

Non-goals

  • Income analysis, or reworking the headline figures for it
  • Budgets over longer periods; YNAB-style budgeting
  • Saved views or named filter sets
  • Automatic exchange rates
  • Changes to the home dashboard or Reports

Anti-goals

  • A page that jerks around when filters open
  • A panel that closes on every pick
  • Losing quick month-to-month stepping
  • A new colour palette, or a bespoke transaction editor

Constraints

  • One transaction editor. Breakdown rows open the existing TransactionDrawer through useTransactionDrawer, as today (PR #42).
  • The date rule (src/lib/dates.ts): transaction dates are calendar days, never converted. "Today" is the local date via localDay(). Every period and comparison boundary is a calendar day.
  • Same counting rules as today: transfers out, split parents out, cash-flow accounts only, returns netted against their purchase, credits netted against their category, everything in base currency. The chart, figures and breakdown must agree to the dollar.
  • Design tokens only (src/app/globals.css), light and dark. The drawer pushes at ≥1100px as today.
  • dashboardAnalytics also serves the home dashboard, Reports and the trailing-12-months report. Their behaviour must not change.
  • DRY > YAGNI > SOLID. Tests never touch prisma/dev.db; use tests/helpers/db.ts.

Rules

  • The current period ends at today, never in the future. › is disabled on the current period.
  • Switching kind keeps you near where you were: Year → Month lands on the current month if it's this year, otherwise December of that year; Month or 3 months → Year keeps the year.
  • Default on a fresh visit: Month, the current month M6.

4 · Filter panel

A floating card, top-right, inset from the edges, rounded with a soft shadow. It slides and fades in from the right, growing from the button's corner A3.

  • Sections, top to bottom, each with a small heading showing what's picked:
    • Period: pills Month · 3 months · Year · Custom. Custom shows two date fields.
    • Compared with: pills, as in section 3.
    • Categories: search, then each group with a tick box (all / some / none) and its categories indented below, each group showing its total for the period. Ticking a group ticks all its categories. Uncategorized last M3.
    • Accounts, Tags: tick lists M1.
    • Merchant: "name contains", case-insensitive.
  • Footer: Clear all · Done.
  • Closing A4: Done, ✕, Esc, or a click outside. Picks apply immediately.
  • Floats over the page; nothing re-flows. The headline figures and the chart's left side stay visible while you pick.
  • Opening a transaction closes the panel; the panel and the transaction drawer are never open together.
  • Chips under the title: "Category Golf +2", each with ✕ to clear that filter. Period and comparison chips have no ✕.
  • Adding a filter later = one more section here, one more chip kind, one more server filter A6.

5 · Over-time chart

Lines, replacing the mock's earlier bars A9. Hovering shows the values at that day or month.

Under two months: "Spending so far"

  • A running total by day across the whole month. This period is a solid accent line with a light fill, ending in a dot at today, with a dashed "Today" marker.
  • The comparison is a dashed muted line running to its month end, so you see where last month finished.
  • A sentence above it: "$3,470 so far · Oct was at $3,558 by this day (−$88) and ended at $5,425." For a finished month: "$4,980 in Oct 2026 · Sep was $5,210."

Two months or more: "Trend"

  • One point per calendar month, with the comparison dashed and lined up month for month.
  • "Per month / Running total" switch M8: running total turns it into so-far-this-period.

"Total / Each category"

  • With 2–6 picks M4, "Each category" draws one line per pick (a fully ticked group is one pick), with each total in the legend. The comparison line is hidden in this view.

6 · Where it went

  • Today's "Spending Distribution" pie and "Top Categories" list merged into one card: a ring and the top five categories with bars and amounts, plus "Everything else".
  • Follows the period and filters.
  • With 2+ category picks it shows the picks, so each slice matches its line; otherwise the top five categories M7.

7 · Breakdown by group

  • As today, grouped by parent category, now with columns: Category · this period · comparison · Change (red up, green down). Only categories with spending in either period show.
  • A row opens to its transactions for the period, respecting every filter. A transaction opens the shared drawer, which steps through that list. Today's held-row behaviour stays.
  • Budget bars only for a single month with no account, tag or merchant filter M5 A12.
  • Today's per-category stats row (This Month / Last Month / Change / Avg) becomes This period / Comparison / Change / Avg per transaction.

8 · Engineering

DRY > YAGNI > SOLID.

Found while checking: two server filters look broken on SQLiteIn buildWhere (src/lib/analytics.ts), tags use array_contains but Transaction.tags is a String? column holding JSON, and merchant uses mode: 'insensitive', which Prisma's SQLite connector doesn't support. Nothing sends these today. Confirm with a failing test first, then fix in this work.

DRY: one source for each rule

  • One period module (e.g. src/lib/analytics-period.ts), pure: from kind + anchor + today, it gives the range, the comparison range, labels, stepping and "is current". The page, the chips and the API query all use it. Today comes from localDay().
  • One spend calculation. Pull the per-transaction "how much does this count as spending" logic out of dashboardAnalytics so the figures, breakdown and new daily/monthly series all use it and can't disagree.
  • One filter rule for tags and merchant, shared by buildWhere and applyFilters, which disagree today (any vs all).
  • Reuse PeriodPicker: add a 3-month mode and a custom label, rather than a second picker. Travel keeps using it unchanged.
  • One colour function: given what's on screen ranked by spend, it hands out PIE_COLORS in order. The lines and the ring both call it M7.
  • The filter editors are one component per filter kind, used only by the panel.

The API

  • /api/analytics/dashboard gains an optional explicit comparison range. Without it, it keeps today's "same length just before", so the home dashboard, Reports and trailing-12 are untouched.
  • It also returns what the chart needs: spending per day (period under two months) or per month, for this period and the comparison, and per pick when "Each category" is on. Whether that's this endpoint or a sibling is left to the builder.
  • Category filters send category ids; a group is sent as its categories.

YAGNI: cut

  • Saved views, filter presets, income.
  • The other layouts in the mock (filter bar, side panel, drop-down).
  • A "pushes content" mode for the panel.
  • An abstraction for "filter kinds" beyond a list the panel renders. Four kinds don't need a plugin system.

Left to the builder

  • Component split, file layout, and whether the chart series is a new endpoint.
  • The line chart: Recharts via ChartRenderer's line type, or a small dedicated component, as long as it matches the mock.
  • URL parameter names M6.
  • Empty states: nothing matches the filters; a period before your history starts.
  • Narrow-width behaviour of the panel (full-width sheet is fine).
